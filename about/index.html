My story

What I enjoy most is taking something that works only because one person remembers how, and turning it into something a whole team can rebuild from Git. Most of that work is on Red Hat platforms (OpenShift, Ansible Automation Platform, RHEL), AWS and Azure, and on clusters I build on premises with VMware or KVM.

Day to day, I'm the technical lead on Red Hat OpenShift (ROSA, ARO) and the escalation point for highly secured environments. I also design and review AWS and Azure architectures, with reliability and cost in mind.

How I work

Everything in Git

If a platform can't be rebuilt from a repository on a bad day, it isn't finished. Configuration, pipelines and documentation all live in version control.

Try it before recommending it

I run a home lab where I test designs on real hardware first, so what I propose has already been rebuilt, and broken, at least once.

Write down the why

A runbook says what to type. I also write down why we made a choice, so the next person can change it without fear.
